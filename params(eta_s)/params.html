How the boost moves the parameters

E = (α1D²/4) sinh 4ηs + E1D cosh 4ηs, α²/4 = (α1D²/8) cosh 4ηs + (E1D/2) sinh 4ηs + α1D²/8, L² the same with the last sign reversed. All three drawn against ηs, with α1D² of either sign.

Parameters

The vertical scale is fixed, not fitted to the curves, so changing α1D² or E1D moves the curves rather than the axis. They leave the frame once the boost is large enough.

What to look at

The centre ηr

G = α²/4 + L² and E obey G² − E² = 𝑴², so the pair is a vector on that hyperbola and its rapidity is the centre of oscillation: tanh 4ηr = E/G when 𝑴² > 0, and G/E when 𝑴² < 0. The boost only translates it, ηr = ηs + const, and the white mark is the boost that brings it to zero. Differentiating gives E′ = 4G and (α²/4)′ = (L²)′ = 2E, so that mark is exactly where E is stationary when 𝑴² < 0, and where the other two are stationary, E crossing zero, when 𝑴² > 0. E itself has no stationary point there, since G² = E² + 𝑴² cannot vanish.

Invariants

α²/4 − L² = α1D²/4 holds at every ηs, and (α²/4 + L²)² − E² = 𝑴² = (α1D²/4)² − E1D². The first is why the two blue and amber curves keep a constant gap; the second is the invariant of the boost.
