Duffing route vs direct integration

Two independent numerical solutions of the same one-dimensional lemniscatic oscillator, sharing only the parameters α1D2 and E1D.

Branch

Below the barrier U(x) has two allowed regions, exchanged by x → 1/x; they are the two wells of the Duffing potential.

Route A — Duffing

Integrates φ̈ = −8φ3 + 2α1D2φ from φ(0)=F+, φ̇(0)=0, then sets x2 = (F+−φ)/(F++φ).

Route B — direct

Integrates the Euler–Lagrange equation of ℒ = ½m(x)(ẋ2 − α1D2x2), m(x)=1/(1+x4), from x(0)=0, ẋ(0)=√(2E1D). The two routes share no intermediate quantity.
